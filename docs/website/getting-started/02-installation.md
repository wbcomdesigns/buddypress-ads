# Installation and Your First Ad

## Install

1. Go to **Plugins > Add New** and upload the plugin zip, or search for **BuddyPress Ads**.
2. Click **Install Now**, then **Activate**.
3. A new **WB Ads** menu appears in the admin sidebar. The plugin settings live under **WB Plugins > BuddyPress Ads**.

## Publish your first ad

1. Go to **WB Ads > Add New WB Ads** and give the ad a title. Visitors never see the title, except as the alt text of an image ad.
2. Under **WB Ads Type**, pick **Plain Text and Code**, **Rich Content** or **Image Ad**.
3. Fill in the ad content under **Ads Parameter**.
4. Under **Display Conditions**, choose where the ad shows. See [Ad Settings](../settings/01-ad-settings.md).
5. Click **Publish**.

New ads are switched on. You can switch any ad off from the **Enable/Disable Ads** column on the **WB Ads** list, and it stays off when you edit and update it later.

An ad with no content cannot be published. It is saved as a draft with a notice.
